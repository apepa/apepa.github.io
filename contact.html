Contact & Opportunities

Opportunities

Funded PhD and postdoc position available! I am a co-PI on a DFF-Research Project 2 grant for "A Mechanistic Framework for Mitigating the Susceptibility of LLMs to Learning False Information". See more info and application details.

Postdoctoral Opportunities

I am interested in supporting prospective postdocs for fellowships such as the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship or Villum International Postdoc. Please, reach out with a draft research statement if you'd like to explore opportunities together.

If you're considering this, please contact me early enough and no later than a month before the deadline.

Visiting Opportunities

Although I do not have direct funding for visitors, I am happy to support applications for DDSA visiting grants, which are particularly suitable if you would like to later apply for a DDSA PhD/Postdoc fellowship with me.

There are also potential opportunities for funding of visits of senior researchers.

Student Supervision

I am happy to supervise Bachelor and Master students for their thesis projects at the University of Copenhagen. Get in touch if you're interested in discussing potential thesis topics. Please, send a list of taken courses and grades with your introduction email.

Teaching Courses

I am currently employed at the University of Copenhagen with specific teaching duties dedicated to life-long learning courses. If you are a public or private entity in Denmark that would like to learn more about Machine Learning, Language Models, Data Science, etc., please reach out.
