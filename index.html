I am an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) in the Department of Computer Science, AI Section at the University of Copenhagen. I co-lead the CopeNLU group.

Research

Interpretability of Language Models

Understanding mechanisms of LLMs with applications to context usage and parametric knowledge.

Explainability Methods

Designing robust and user-aligned explainability techniques for complex models.

Factuality in LMs

Addressing the challenge of maintaining factual accuracy in language models.
